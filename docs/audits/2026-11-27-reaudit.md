## 2. New findings (survived skeptics)

### N-01 M-04 fix bypassed by chunked minting (Medium)

- **Locations:** `src/NoUSDPositionManager.sol:182-184`, `:470-476` (`_distribute`); `src/NoUSD.sol:297-320` (`distribute`); `test/AuditRegressions.t.sol:74` (single-mint test).
- **Attack:** with `mintFeeBps > 0` and a small honest eligible float E0, the attacker mints total A in growing chunks in one tx. Each chunk's fee is distributed over eligible supply that already contains the attacker's earlier chunks, so chunk i refunds M_i/(E0+M_i) of its fee. `burn` then pays the refund back through `consumeAccrued`. Fee actually left for holders ≈ fee_rate · E0 · ln(1 + A/E0) instead of fee_rate · A. Needs no outside noUSD liquidity, so it works from launch.
- **PoC** (bob 1k eligible, 50 bps, 100k total): single mint refunds 0. Eight doubling mints refund 467.8 of 499.4 (93.7%). 24 mints at 1.3x refund 94.8%. Chunking also gives more noUSD (99,373 vs 99,217, less price impact). Extra cost is ~0.6-0.7M gas per mint.
- **Impact:** the effective mint fee falls from 50 bps to ~3 bps at E0/A = 1/100, and to ~0 at launch. The §11 mint-side lever against M-02 still does not work. No unbacked noUSD or claims.
- **Fix (owner decision, changes spec §9.1 step 8):** route the mint fee to `treasuryAccrued`, or stream it / pay it only to balances held since the previous sync (the same mechanism as the M-03 fix). At minimum, add a chunked case to the M-04 test.
- **Skeptics:** both upheld it at Medium. Notes: zero impact at the launch `mintFeeBps = 0`. It reopens M-04 under the same conditional framing and shares the M-03 root cause (a zero-duration balance earns pro-rata). The refund is ~$468 per 100k at 50 bps (the finder's "$47" was 10x low). Refund share: ~94% at E0/A = 1/100, 31% at E0 = A, 5% at E0 = 10A.

### N-02 Free self-donation steers pool selection into a thin pool, so mints revert `CapExceeded` (Low)

- **Locations:** `src/lib/PoolSelection.sol:72-84` (`_score`), `:101-111` (`_yield`); `src/NoUSDPositionManager.sol:631-633` (post-add cap, no fallback), `:494-501` (`_rotateObs`).
- **Attack:** in an Active pool X where the manager has no in-range liquidity and outside liquidity is thin, the attacker becomes the only LP in range with dust. Inside one unlock it calls `donate(X, D)` and collects D back via `modifyLiquidity(0)`, so the net cost is ~1 wei. `feeGrowthGlobal` jumps, `L_pool` cancels out of the score, X beats every deep pool, and the `lOurs == 0` pre-check passes. Honest mints of X's tokens add to X and then fail the post-add share cap. The inflated growth persists for 1-2 `selectionWindow`s and can be repeated.
- **PoC:** third pool A/B at 5 bps, attacker L = 8e14, D = 100 A. B 10k, A 10k and A 1M mints all revert, and still do after 7 days of churn. A C mint works.
- **Impact:** mint DoS for one pair's tokens, plus free steering of mints into a chosen pool (sets up L-01/L-06). No funds at risk. Undone by `retirePool(X)` or `setPoolWeight(X, 0)`.
- **Fix:** in `_score`, skip a candidate when `(lOurs + L_new) * 10000 > (lPool + L_new) * maxShareBps`, or require a minimum `lPool`. Make `feeYield` hard to game: measure only the manager's own in-range growth, or cap per-pool yield relative to the median.
- **Skeptics:** split. Upheld at Low (reproduced). Refuting skeptic: not exploitable at launch. The Ethereum launch set has disjoint pairs (one Active pool per token), so there is nothing to steer into. Launch pools are deep ($596k / $861k), where a donation is not free. Small mints fill X to its cap, after which selection falls back. This becomes relevant once overlapping pairs over a thin pool are activated (e.g. Arbitrum USDC/USDT0 next to DAI/USDT0). Treat as Low, conditional on pool configuration.

### N-03 L-04 fix incomplete: a matured request with high `minOut` is a standing instant exit for any `burnDelay > 0` (Low)

- **Locations:** `src/NoUSDPositionManager.sol:247-257` (`requestBurn`), `:514-525` (`_executeOne`), `:261-277` (`executeBurns` skips on `Slippage`); `src/lib/BurnQueue.sol:155-167`; `docs/spec.md:585`, `:588`.
- **Attack:** with `burnDelay > 0`, the attacker parks `requestBurn(N, minOut)` with `minOut` a few bps above the at-par payout. Keepers skip it, and it matures after one wait and never expires. The at-par value of an in-kind removal is convex and lowest at par, so during an in-band depeg it clears `minOut`. In one tx the attacker mints N at par with the weak token and calls `executeBurn(id)`, receiving a healthy basket. PoC: 7-day delay, 10k parked for 68 days with two keeper runs that skip it, then mint + execute in one sequence.
- **Impact:** `burnDelay` never constrains pre-positioned capital, so the M-02/L-04 edge (~18 bps of N per episode) returns for every depeg. The cost is forgone escrow yield.
- **Fix (owner decision, changes §9.3):** an execution window (after `readyAt + EXEC_WINDOW` only cancel is possible, triggerable by anyone), optionally plus effective `readyAt = max(stored, createdAt + burnDelay)`. Correct spec §11: moving the price raises, not lowers, a removal's at-par value. Add a harm-level regression test with `burnDelay > 0` from the start.
- **Skeptics:** split. Upheld at Low. Refuting skeptic: inert at launch (`burnDelay = 0`, so `requestBurn` reverts). All profit comes from the open M-02. The option is single-use and pays only if in-band depegs come more often than ~2-3 weeks of forgone yield. Any keeper can execute first once `minOut` is reachable. Treat as hardening required before any `burnDelay` raise.

### N-04 `executeBurns` cost unbounded by `n`: done entries behind a pinned head are not counted (Low)

- **Locations:** `src/NoUSDPositionManager.sol:261-277` (`if (r.done) continue;` runs before `++visited`); `src/lib/BurnQueue.sol:180-184` (`advance` stops at the first not-done entry); `:293-298` (`cancelBurn` refunds in full).
- **Attack:** with `burnDelay > 0`, the attacker pins `queueHead` with a 1-wei request at `minOut = type(uint128).max`, then repeats request + cancel K times. Every `executeBurns(n)` rescans all K done entries at ~2.5k gas each (measured: 1.45-1.65M gas at K=500, 5.2-5.4M at K=2000). At K ≈ 12-15k it exceeds the block / per-tx gas limit. Spam costs ~211k gas per entry: cheap on L2, expensive on mainnet. Out-of-order executes and cancels also grow the scan without any attacker.
- **Impact:** the batch keeper path reverts permanently for every caller. `executeBurn(id)` and `burn` stay O(1), so funds are safe. This disproves the 2026-11-26 L-05 skeptic note that the stall cost is bounded by `n`.
- **Fix:** count every scanned entry toward `n`, or keep a separate persistent scan cursor. Adopt L-05's minimum request size and/or request expiry so one junk request cannot pin the head.
- **Skeptics:** split. Upheld at Low. Refuting skeptic: inert at launch (`burnDelay = 0`), no attacker profit, the 84:1 gas ratio favours the defender, spec §9.3 documents "visits up to n not-done requests", and the root cause is L-05. Treat as an addendum to L-05.

## 3. New claims refuted

None was refuted outright: all four candidates got at least one upholding skeptic. Downgrade claims that were rejected:

- N-01 "duplicate of M-03": rejected. It needs no outside flash liquidity and works from launch.
- N-02 "not exploitable": holds only for the Ethereum launch set (disjoint pairs). Reproduced on overlapping pools.
- N-03 "informational, restates M-02": rejected. The profit is M-02's, but the delay bypass is new and contradicts L-04's "Fixed".
- N-04 "informational, duplicate of L-05": rejected. Done entries are uncounted, so the cost is not bounded by `n`.
